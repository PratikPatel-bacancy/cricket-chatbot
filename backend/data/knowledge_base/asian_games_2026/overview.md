# 2026 Asian Games — Overview

## Host and dates

The 2026 Asian Games (the 20th edition) are hosted by Aichi and Nagoya, Japan, running from
September 19 to October 4, 2026. Some sports, including men's basketball, begin competition
earlier than the official opening, with basketball starting on September 10, 2026.

This is the third time Japan has hosted the Asian Games, following Tokyo in 1958 and Hiroshima in
1994.

## Organizing body

The Asian Games are organized by the Olympic Council of Asia (OCA), the governing body for
sport in Asia, held once every four years.

## Scale of the event

Aichi-Nagoya 2026 features 43 sports, 71 disciplines, and 469 medal events. More than 11,000
athletes from 45 countries and regions across Asia are expected to compete.

## Related event

A separate 2026 Asian Para Games is also held in the same host region, for athletes with
disabilities, following the same general multi-sport-event model as the Asian Games.

## Scope note

This knowledge base covers only fixed, pre-announced facts about the event (host, dates, format,
and sports program) — not live results, medal tallies, schedules for individual matches, or
athlete rosters, since those change continuously during the Games and cannot be kept accurate in
a static reference document.
