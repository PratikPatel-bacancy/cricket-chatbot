# 2026 Asian Games — Sports Program

## Sports making their Asian Games debut

Five sports are appearing at the Asian Games for the first time at Aichi-Nagoya 2026:

- **Padel**
- **Teqball**
- **Surfing**
- **BMX Freestyle**
- **Mixed Martial Arts (MMA)**

## Sports retained from the previous edition

**Breaking** and **esports** both made their official Asian Games debut at the 2023 Hangzhou
Asian Games and have been retained for the 2026 program in Aichi-Nagoya.

## Olympic-programme sports included

The Asian Games program includes many sports that are also contested at the Olympic Games, such
as athletics, swimming, badminton, boxing, basketball, and field hockey, among others.

## Sports with a strong Asian identity

Alongside Olympic sports, the Asian Games traditionally include sports with particular popularity
or origin in Asia, such as:

- **Kabaddi**
- **Sepaktakraw**
- **Wushu**
- **Kurash**
- **Jiu-jitsu**

## Overall numbers

Across all disciplines, the 2026 Asian Games program totals 43 sports, 71 disciplines, and 469
medal events (see the overview document for host, dates, and participant numbers).
