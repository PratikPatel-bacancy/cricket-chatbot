# Penalties and Penalty Shootouts

## Taking a penalty kick during normal play

The ball must be stationary on the penalty spot, the kicker must be clearly identified, and all
players other than the kicker and the defending goalkeeper must be outside the penalty area and
the penalty arc, at least 9.15m (10 yards) from the ball, until it is kicked. The goalkeeper must
have at least part of one foot on or in line with the goal line, facing the kicker, and cannot
move off the line before the ball is kicked (minor movement along the line is allowed, but they
cannot come forward off the line early).

## Re-taking a penalty

If the goalkeeper encroaches illegally and the kick is missed or saved, the kick is retaken. If an
attacking teammate encroaches into the area early and the kick is scored, it is retaken as an
indirect free kick to the defending team instead in some cases, or the goal may stand depending on
who encroached and the outcome — the exact retake/award outcome depends on which side encroached
and whether the kick was scored, saved, or missed.

## Penalty shootouts

Used to determine a winner when a knockout match is level after normal time and extra time (where
extra time is played). Each team takes alternating kicks from the penalty spot, taken by different
players, until one team has scored more goals than the other could possibly match with their
remaining kicks. If still level after 5 kicks each, the shootout continues in "sudden death"
rounds — one kick each per round — until one team scores and the other misses.

## Rules specific to shootouts

- Only players who were on the field at the end of the match (or eligible substitutes who came on)
  may take part.
- A goalkeeper can be substituted for another eligible player between kicks if injured.
- Each kick is taken under the same conditions as a normal penalty kick (stationary ball,
  goalkeeper on the line until struck).
