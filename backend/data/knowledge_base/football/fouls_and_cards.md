# Fouls and Cards

## Direct free kick fouls

A direct free kick (or penalty, if inside the offender's own penalty area) is awarded if a player
commits any of the following against an opponent, in a manner the referee considers careless,
reckless, or using excessive force: kicking, tripping, jumping at, charging, striking, pushing, or
tackling an opponent in a way that plays the opponent rather than the ball. Handling the ball
deliberately (including making the body unnaturally bigger to block it) is also a direct free
kick offense.

## Indirect free kick fouls

An indirect free kick is awarded for offenses such as: dangerous play that doesn't involve
physical contact, obstructing an opponent's progress without playing the ball, preventing the
goalkeeper from releasing the ball from their hands, or a goalkeeper handling a deliberate
back-pass from a teammate's foot, or handling the ball again after releasing it before it touches
another player.

## Yellow card (caution)

A yellow card is shown for offenses including: unsporting behavior, dissent by word or action,
persistent infringement of the Laws, delaying the restart of play, failing to respect the required
distance on a free kick or corner, entering or leaving the field without the referee's permission,
or deliberate handball to stop a promising attack in some circumstances.

## Red card (sending off)

A red card, resulting in the player being sent off and their team playing with one fewer player
for the rest of the match, is shown for: serious foul play, violent conduct, spitting at anyone,
denying an obvious goal-scoring opportunity by a handball or a foul (unless it's the goalkeeper in
their own area handling the ball, or the foul is a genuine attempt to play the ball), using
offensive or abusive language or gestures, or receiving a second yellow card in the same match.

## Penalty kick

A penalty kick is awarded when a direct free kick offense is committed by a defending player
inside their own penalty area, regardless of where the ball was at the time. The kick is taken
from the penalty spot (12 yards / 11 meters from goal), with only the kicker and the defending
goalkeeper allowed inside the penalty area and arc until the ball is kicked.
