# Match Structure

## Duration

A standard match consists of two halves of 45 minutes each, separated by a half-time interval of
no more than 15 minutes. The referee adds "added time" (stoppage time) at the end of each half to
compensate for time lost to substitutions, injuries, time-wasting, and other stoppages.

## Extra time and penalties

In knockout competitions, if the match is level after 90 minutes, two periods of extra time (15
minutes each) may be played. If still level after extra time, the match is decided by a penalty
shootout (see the dedicated penalties document).

## Team composition

Each team fields 11 players, including a goalkeeper, at the start of a match. A match cannot
continue if a team has fewer than 7 players. The number of permitted substitutions varies by
competition (commonly 5 substitutions from a named bench in many modern competitions, made within
a limited number of stoppages, excluding half-time).

## Kickoff and restarts

A kickoff starts each half and restarts play after a goal. Other restarts include: throw-ins (when
the ball fully crosses the touchline), goal kicks (when the ball crosses the goal line last touched
by an attacker), corner kicks (when the ball crosses the goal line last touched by a defender), and
free kicks (direct or indirect, following a foul).
