# Scoring and Service

## Rally point scoring

Modern badminton uses rally point scoring: a point is scored on every rally regardless of which
side served, unlike older "side-out" scoring. A match is best-of-3 games, and each game is played
to **21 points**, won by whichever side reaches 21 first with at least a 2-point lead. If the score
reaches 20-20, play continues until one side leads by 2 points, up to a hard cap of **30 points**
(reaching 30 first wins outright even without a 2-point margin).

## Serving rules

The server must strike the shuttle below their waist (below the lowest rib, per the current Laws)
with the racket head pointing downward at the moment of contact, and the entire shuttle must be
below 1.15m from the court surface at the point of impact. Both feet of the server and receiver
must stay in contact with the court (within their service courts) until the serve is struck, and
neither can fake or unduly delay the serve.

## Which side serves and receives

In singles, if the server's score is even, they serve from the right service court; if odd, from
the left. In doubles, the same right/left rule based on the serving side's score applies, and
serves alternate between the two partners on a side as they win server-side rallies, following
specific doubles rotation rules.

## Let (service or rally replay)

A let is called (rally replayed with no point awarded) for situations such as: the shuttle getting
caught on top of the net and remaining there after passing over, a serve being made before the
receiver is ready, or unexpected outside interference during play.

## Winning a match

A match is won by the first side to win 2 games out of 3. If a decisive third game is needed, sides
switch ends when the leading side reaches 11 points in that game.
